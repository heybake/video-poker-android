# Video Poker Android

**Version 1.0.31**

Standalone, offline Video Poker trainer and player for Android (Pixel 10 Pro / Pixel 10 Pro XL).

## Features
- **100% Offline & Self-Contained:** Plays anywhere without internet (airplane mode, travel, couch).
- **41 Built Games:** Imports all approved game JSON configurations from the Workbench.
- **Complete Strategy Priority List:** Full scrollable strategy priority table with real-time hand matches and top-choice highlights.
- **Priority Slot Numbers:** Clean, single priority slot numbers (`Spot X`) on every hold choice.
- **3 Training Modes:**
  - **Learn:** Hold list shows optimal moves with single slot numbers and holds cards automatically.
  - **Practice:** Self-test mode comparing player holds against the optimal strategy table after the draw.
  - **Casino:** Authentic casino simulation with full pay tables and zero hints.
- **Landscape Ergonomics:** Designed for one- and two-thumb play with centered version display and clear bottom controls.
- **Card Swiping / Slide to Hold:** Fluid touch gestures across cards left and right to hold or release.
- **Authentic Cashier Voucher & Full Reset:** Take Voucher resets cash, hand counters, cards, and state to pristine card backs.
- **Local Session Recording:** Keeps full logs matching the Workbench format with live recording indicator.
- **Authentic Sound Effects:** Full sound package for deals, draws, holds, coins, and win fanfares.
